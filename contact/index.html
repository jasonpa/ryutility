contact@ryutility.com

Messages are read, and we try to answer within a few days. There is no phone line and no separate support desk.

What a useful message includes

  • A wrong result — the page address, every input you typed, and the output you saw. The same inputs should match the worked example printed under that calculator.
  • A formula question — which tool, and what you expected instead. Sources are listed on the About page and under each calculator.
  • A correction — quote the sentence on a guide or tool page and the source you are comparing it to.
  • A privacy request — say so in the subject line. What the site collects is described in the Privacy Policy. Calculator inputs stay in your browser.

What this address is not

It is not a coaching service and not a medical consult. Questions about your own training plan belong with a coach or clinician. The Terms of Use explain that calculator output is informational. Messages that ask the site to change a formula so it matches one race result will get an explanation of the formula, not a custom model.
